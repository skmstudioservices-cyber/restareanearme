❓ Frequently asked questions

Where is the best rest area on an Indian highway?

NHAI wayside amenities and 24×7 petrol-pump food courts — lit, staffed and with clean washrooms. See the NH-wise list on this page.

How often are NHAI wayside amenities spaced?

Roughly every 40–60 km on National Highways; 501 have been awarded and 94 are operational.

What facilities are at an NHAI wayside amenity?

Fuel, EV charging, food court, clean toilets, parking, driver dormitory, ATM and a medical clinic.

How often should I stop on a long drive?

At least 15 minutes every 2 hours or 150 km, whichever comes first.

Which highways have the most rest areas?

NH-44 and NH-48 have the densest networks; the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway is adding ~93 stops.

Are rest areas safe at night?

Choose lit, busy places — 24×7 petrol-pump food courts are the safest default.
